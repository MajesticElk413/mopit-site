Common questions

Where are my routines saved?

MopIt stores its chores, notes and completion history locally on your device. It does not currently offer an account-based synchronization service.

How do I change a routine?

Open the Routines tab and tap a routine to change its name, color or recurrence schedule.

How do I remove a routine?

In Routines, swipe a routine left to reveal the delete action. The app is designed to preserve past completion information separately.

What if I replace my iPhone?

MopIt does not currently have its own cloud backup or restore feature. Do not assume routines will automatically transfer to a new device. Your device's own backup settings may affect what can be restored.
