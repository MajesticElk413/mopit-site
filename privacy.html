MopIt privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MopIt, a personal household chore and routine tracking application, handles information in its current iOS release.

1. Who is responsible for this policy?

MopIt is developed and maintained by Ioannis Valindras. For privacy enquiries, contact goalumi.support@gmail.com.

2. Data stored in the app

MopIt lets you enter chore names, recurrence settings, categories, colors, notes and completion dates. This information is saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device app data storage.

3. Data collection and sharing

In the current version, MopIt does not require an account, collect your household data on a developer server, use analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps, or send routine information to us. There is no MopIt-operated cloud synchronization feature.

4. Device backups

Your device's operating system and backup settings may affect whether local app data is included in device backups. MopIt does not currently provide its own online backup, synchronization or cross-device restore service.

5. Support communications

If you voluntarily email goalumi.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, message and attachments. We use it to respond to your request and manage related correspondence. This email communication is separate from data stored inside the app. We process support messages to respond to requests and maintain related correspondence, based on our legitimate interests in providing support (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable). We retain messages only as long as reasonably needed for support and any applicable legal obligations.

6. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. MopIt does not embed third-party advertising or analytics scripts on these pages. GitHub may process technical information about site visits according to its own policies. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Data deletion

You can edit or delete routines and notes through the app where those controls are available. Uninstalling the app typically removes its local data from the device, but information may remain in device backups controlled by your device settings. To request deletion of a support email you sent, contact us at the address above.

8. Children's privacy

MopIt is intended as a general household organization tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

9. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you can request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information that you supplied in support communications, and you may object to processing or raise concerns with your data protection authority. Contact us at the email address below to make a request. Local app records remain on your device and are managed through the app and your device settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If MopIt adds features such as accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics, we will review and update this policy as needed.

11. Contact

For questions about MopIt's privacy practices: goalumi.support@gmail.com.
